# Feedback

Thank you for your interest in Celata. Celata is a small project developed by one person in their spare
time. What helps it most is your feedback: bug reports, ideas for new features and security concerns are
all very welcome.

Please follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) in issues and comments.

## Reporting a bug

Open an issue with the form **Bug report**. It asks for the version of Celata (`celata --version`), your
distribution, the version of the hide.me client and the steps that lead to the problem.

Issues are public. **Do not paste your Access-Token, passwords or logs that show your addresses.**

If your internet is blocked right now, the [emergency exit](README.md#internet-blocked-emergency-exit) at
the top of the README frees it.

Before you report, [Known limitations](README.md#known-limitations) and
[Troubleshooting](README.md#troubleshooting) may already have the answer. Problems of the VPN connection
itself often belong to the hide.me client; its project has
[its own issue tracker](https://github.com/eventure/hide.client.linux/issues).

## Suggesting a feature

Open an issue with the form **Feature request**. Describe the situation you are in and what you are missing;
that helps more than a finished solution. Not every idea can be built, but every idea is read.

## Security concerns

Never report them in a public issue. Report them privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Concerns about the design are welcome too, not only finished attacks.

## Translations

Celata is available in English and German. If a text is wrong, unclear or cut off in your language, open an
issue, quote the text and say where it appears. If you would like Celata in another language, say so in a
feature request.
